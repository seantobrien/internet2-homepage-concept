01 Discover our services

Everything you need to move discovery forward.

Network, trust and identity, and cloud solutions—designed around the needs of research and education.

  • Internet2 Network The national backbone purpose-built for research and education traffic, now carrying 1,600G+ of wavelength capability.
  • InCommon Single sign-on across more than 1,000 participating organizations, so your people reach what they need without a new password.
  • eduroam Secure Wi-Fi that follows your students and staff to thousands of campuses worldwide, with no guest account to request.
  • Certificate Service Unlimited TLS/SSL certificates for every domain your institution owns, at one predictable annual rate.
  • Internet2 NET+ Cloud services evaluated and negotiated by the community, with contract terms and security reviews built for R&E.
  • Cloud Connect Private, high-capacity links straight into the major cloud providers, bypassing the commodity internet entirely.
  • Insight Console Your institution's traffic, capacity and performance across the Internet2 Network, in one place you can actually read.
  • Performance Tools Find the bottleneck before your researchers do, with perfSONAR measurement points across the R&E ecosystem.
  • Cloud Scorecard Compare cloud offerings against accessibility, security and privacy requirements before your institution commits.
  • Trusted Access Platform Open-source identity and access software, including Grouper and Shibboleth, maintained by and for the community.
